## 2025-06-30 - Added title attributes to icon-only buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like the lightbox controls and mobile nav toggle) implemented primarily with SVGs and `aria-label`s fail to provide visual cues (tooltips) for sighted mouse users. This negatively affects discoverability for their functions (Zoom In, Zoom Out, Close, Toggle Navigation).
**Action:** Always ensure that icon-only interactive elements programmatically generated or statically declared pair `aria-label` with a matching `title` attribute so both assistive technologies and hover-enabled visual users receive descriptions.
