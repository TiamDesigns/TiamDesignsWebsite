01 // SYSTEM OVERVIEW & BACKGROUND

I am an Electrical Engineering student at the University of Ottawa with a background in Industrial Design. I build practical embedded hardware, custom microcontroller boards, and prototype electronics. Combining board design with physical enclosure layout allows me to take projects smoothly from initial schematics to finished physical devices.

My experience focuses on C/C++ firmware development, multi-layer PCB design, and hands-on hardware debugging. I'm also actively building my digital logic depth—moving from basic CPU architectures up to custom RISC-V design on FPGAs.
